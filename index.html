How Australia Changed In A Decade

age, education, employment, population, family structure, language and income across a decade of Census data.

Australia changed in many ways between 2011 and 2021. These visualisations explore where those changes happened, which groups experienced them, and how the patterns differ across the country.

Age Growth (2011 - 2021)

Median Age Growth Across Australia

Across Australia while median age did not always change from 2011 to 2016,
All median age increased by at least 1 year by 2021.

Age Groups Across Australia

While growth was not equal among different age-groups, every age group
showed some growth across the decade.

Secondary & Tertiary Education Growth (2011 - 2021)

(%) Australians With Bachelors or Above By State

Among all states, ACT has the highest percentage of people with
either a bachelor or above certification out of people with qualifications (60.1%).

Data shows the percentage of qualification-holders aged 15+ with a bachelor's
degree or higher certificate per state (excludes individuals without qualifications).

(%) Australians That Have Completed Secondary Education

Education levels have improved across the decade with Year 12 completion increasing
by (8.7%) from 52% to 60.7% and other levels decreasing.

Data includes equivalent education levels for year levels 8 and above.

How Australia's Industries have evolved (2011 - 2021)

Industry Employment Out of All Australian Industries (%)

Among the eight largest industries, Health Care saw the biggest growth (+2.93%) and
Manufacturing the biggest decline (-3.05%) in their share of total employment across all
industries.

Employment Share (%) was calculated using total number of employees
from all industries.

Total Employee Share (%) of Broad Industries

The Public & Community Services sector has shown
the biggest growth in employment share (4.26%) while the Trade Transport &
Hospitality sector has shown the biggest decline (-2.58%)

The number of individual industries grouped within each category varies. Share percentages
reflect the collective size of the structural group, not an equal distribution of sectors

Family & Population Change (2011 - 2021)

Family Structure Change (%)

Across the decade, couples with children declined (-0.9%) and Couples without
children increased (+1.0%) with other family types showing marginal changes like
(-0.1%) or no changes.

Population Change Australia (%)

While some locations like the outback did not see much population change
besides slight declines in Queensland & South Australia (-3.7%), city regions like Melbourne
saw large increases in population across the decade of up to (38.5%).

How Earnings & Family Incomes Have Changed (2011 - 2021)

Top 5 Industries By Average Weekly Earnings ($)

Across the decade, Mining has remained at the top of earnings with
Financial & Insurance Services and Information Media entering the top 5 from
2016 onwards.

Median Weekly Family Income ($) Equivalised By State

Median family income has shown growth throughout every state, with
ACT having the highest across the different years and
having the largest increase of an additonal ($374) from 2011

Language Growth (2011 - 2021) & Conclusion

Number Of Second Language Speakers

Across the years, the number of people in Australia that can
speak multiple languages have increased.

Conclusion

The Census offers several connected views of change: where population and age grew, how education and employment evolved, how family structures shifted, how language has grown and how income differs by place. Read together, these patterns show how Australia changed throughout a decade through both broad trends and local differences.
